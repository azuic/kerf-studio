A wall gap is a long narrow slot that punches through both side walls but stops above the floor. A bayonet set stacks a shaft hole, two lug notches and a wider groove below — the matching pin drops in and twists 90° to lock.

Clearance / side
The insert copies the selected hole’s shape shrunk by the clearance on every side, so it snaps or twist-locks into the printed cavity. 0.20 mm suits a P1S with 0.4 mm nozzle; use 0.10–0.15 for press fits, 0.30 for free rotation.
